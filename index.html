About us

FUNMeC is led by Prof. Hassan Ait Ahsaine at the Department of Chemistry, Faculty of Science, Mohammed V University in Rabat, Morocco. We design and study functional materials, including vanadates, MXenes, metal-organic frameworks and porphyrins, for photocatalysis, photoelectrochemical water splitting, hydrogen evolution and CO₂ reduction.

Research

Photocatalysis

Vanadate and BiVO₄-based photocatalysts for the degradation of dyes and pollutants.

Photoelectrochemical water splitting

Nanostructured oxide photoelectrodes and the role of oxygen vacancies and sacrificial agents.

Electrocatalysis

MXenes and nickel MOF derivatives for the hydrogen evolution reaction.

CO₂ reduction

Molecular catalysts such as iron porphyrins for selective electrochemical CO₂ reduction.
